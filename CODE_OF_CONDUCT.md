# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful. Critique ideas, not people. Assume good faith. Disagree with technical anchors, not with insults.

## Unacceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information
- Trolling or sustained disruption

## Enforcement

Report violations to `cristian.raduoltean@gmail.com`. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, or contributors that violate this code.
